Operation Sindoor was a series of targeted, precision military strikes launched by the Indian Armed Forces on May 7–8, 2025.

Key Facts

• Trigger: A terrorist attack in Pahalgam, Jammu and Kashmir, on April 22, 2025, which killed 26 civilians (perpetrated by The Resistance Front, an offshoot of Lashkar-e-Taiba).
• Targets: Nine terrorist infrastructure and training sites located inside Pakistan and Pakistan-Occupied Kashmir (PoK), including hubs in Muridke, Bahawalpur, and Muzaffarabad. No Pakistani military or civilian facilities were targeted.
• Execution: Carried out as a coordinated tri-service operation using indigenous defense systems like Akash missiles, precision munitions, and electronic warfare units, completing the primary strikes in roughly 22 minutes.
• Indus Waters Treaty: Alongside the strikes, India announced the suspension of the 1960 Indus Waters Treaty with immediate effect pending a halt to cross-border terrorism.
• Strategic Shift: The action established a stricter counter-terrorism posture, signaling that cross-border attacks will face direct conventional responses rather than symbolic measures
