Operation Mahadev was a joint counter-terrorism mission carried out on July 28, 2025, by the Indian Army's Chinar Corps (including Para commandos and Rashtriya Rifles), the Jammu and Kashmir Police, and the CRPF in the Lidwas area of the Dachigam forest near Srinagar.

Key Details

• Objective: Hunt down and eliminate the perpetrators of the April 22, 2025, terror attack in Pahalgam (Baisaran Valley), which resulted in the deaths of 26 civilians.
• Eliminated Terrorists: Security forces neutralised three senior Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) operatives during the intense firefight:
	• Suleiman (Suleman Shah alias Hashim Musa): Identified as a top LeT commander and the primary mastermind behind the Pahalgam and Gagangir attacks.
	• Hamza Afghani: Senior LeT operative.
	• Jibran: LeT operative also linked to the previous year's Sonamarg tunnel attack.
• Official Recognition: Union Home Minister Amit Shah detailed the success of the mission in the Lok Sabha during discussions on Operation Sindoor, praising the coordinated efforts of the security forces.
